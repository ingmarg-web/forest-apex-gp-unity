# Forest Apex GP — migration reference

The Unity version should preserve the established Forest Apex GP identity rather than become an unrelated racing prototype.

## Golden-reference systems

- analogue steering joystick on the left;
- independent throttle and brake buttons;
- accessible arcade/simcade handling;
- three selectable cars;
- three existing track identities;
- garage -> car select -> event select -> race -> results -> garage flow;
- AI drivers with different lines / profiles;
- overtaking and defending behaviour;
- speed-dependent chase camera and FOV;
- Android-first performance.

## Track identity

### Pine Ridge
Mountain / forest race. Narrower, technical and rhythm-driven, with elevation, hairpins, trees and rock faces.

### Sunset Gulch
Fast desert / canyon circuit with long sight lines, broad sweepers, drafting and warm sunset atmosphere.

### Neon Downtown
Urban night showcase track with dense city dressing, emissive lighting and a large bridge that is a real race section. The player climbs onto it, races across the high point and descends into another city district.

## Product target

Move toward a small but genuinely polished premium mobile racer. New systems should improve driving, presentation, replayability, identity or polish rather than merely increasing feature count.
